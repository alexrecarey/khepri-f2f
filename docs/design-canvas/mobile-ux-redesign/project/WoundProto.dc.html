Try it
Hover a bar segment — its row lights, the rest dim; tooltip gives exactly / at least.
Hover a row — only the segments that count towards it stay lit ("at least", shown).
Tap ACTIVE / REACTIVE WINS — fades the other side.

Shock — against a 1 VITA trooper without Shock immunity, any wound kills: Unconscious, NWI and Dogged are skipped, so its list is just Dead with a Shock tag. STR, 2+ VITA and Shock-immune targets are unaffected.
