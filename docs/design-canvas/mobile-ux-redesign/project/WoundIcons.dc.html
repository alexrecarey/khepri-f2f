A — Icons in the segments
Each state segment carries its glyph in ink colour; plain wounds become pips (one dot per wound). Glyphs drop out when a segment is narrower than 18 px.
B — Pips only
No glyphs: dots count how far down the ladder that segment is (1 dot = 1 wound … 5 = dead). Language-free, but needs the list to decode.
C — Legend under the bar
2W1W
The bar stays clean; the glyphs sit under each segment in the side colour. Never cramped, works at any width.
D — Hatched states
Unconscious states are striped, Dead is the deepest solid. You read "out of action" from texture, no icons needed; icons stay in the list.
E — Threshold marks
Only the lines that matter: where Dead starts and where Unconscious starts, with the glyph above. Fastest read of "how often is it out of the fight".
